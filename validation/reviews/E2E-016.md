**R-06: Raster-image `inline` download disposition is beyond FILES' requirements and behaves differently on local and Cloudflare.** Blocking. Owner: FILES (D-02 for files.md and the Cloudflare part; B-11's `src/files/service.ts`).
FILES-REQ-001…006 (files.md:9-49) say nothing about download names or disposition. DEC-0313 (decision-log.md:2433-2436) itself says "files.md does not say what name a download gets". Naming the download after the file fills that gap. Choosing `inline` instead of `attachment` for raster images is an added behaviour that no requirement asks for. As implemented, it also diverges by target:
- local: `files/local/route.ts:46` passes the stored content type, so raster images download `inline`;
- Cloudflare: `files/service.ts:43,150` calls `tickets.download(name)` with no content type, so `files/cloudflare/tickets.ts:83` always signs `attachment`. files.md:65 documents this ("`inline` only when the service passes the file's content type … `attachment` otherwise"), and DEC-0329 (decision-log.md:2591) leaves the service change open ("Until then, Cloudflare names raster images `attachment`").

Both DEC-0313 and DEC-0329 are "owner review open". Resolution belongs to the owning items. One option is to drop the raster-`inline` branch so every target sends `attachment; filename*=…`. The other is to have the owner accept the behaviour as a requirement and complete the `service.ts` wiring so the targets agree. Related: DEC-0313's status line (decision-log.md:2434) still says "not yet implemented", which is stale (disposition.ts:30 exists).

**R-07: `remove --confirm-production <s>` is an option CLI-REQ-001 and PROV-REQ-003 do not list.** Non-blocking. Owner: D-05 (cli.md §2 text at cli.md:87; `cli/commands/remove.ts:2,29`).
CLI-REQ-001 (cli.md:22) and PROV-REQ-003 specify `remove --target <t> --stage <s>` for "the named stage". It stays open until the owner accepts it or chooses "refuse production stages entirely", as DEC-0350 itself asks.

**R-08: `generate-migration [--name <slug>]` is an option CLI-REQ-001 does not list.** Non-blocking. Owner: B-17 (cli.md:83; `cli/main.ts:39`) and B-07 (DEC-0142, migrations.md:41).

**R-09: `PROTOTYPE_FRAMEWORK_ABORT_AT` is a runner-only hook in the shipped `deploy` command.** Non-blocking (traced). Owner: D-05 (`cli/commands/deploy.ts:5,42`).
