  • We Have GitOps at Home: How Meta Went from Terraform on Cron to GitOps for AI Infra Nov 11, 2026 • 10:11 AM-10:16 AM Salt Palace | Level 1 | Hall DE

    At Meta, ~15 engineers run ~100 Kubernetes clusters for AI research & inference. Terraform exceeds expectations at its job: setting up infra for those clusters. The problem is its side gig: deploying the k8s apps. Even a one-line Helm values change can take a working day in our monolithic Terraform pipeline, where one stuck app can block dozens of others. In the new model, Terraform didn't get fired, just reorged. Kargo and Argo CD take over the apps, so each app ships on its own and a routine update is live and healthy in minutes.
